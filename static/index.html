About Us

We are a community-based event organizing team dedicated to competitive fighting games and the people that love them.

Principles

These are the guiding principles of our organization. The criteria by which we decide which course of action to pursue and which projects to prioritize.

01

Enjoy Fighting Games

At its core, we come together to celebrate and enjoy fighting games. To that end, we seek to make a space where fighting game players can find new friends and rivals. Whether it be playing the games, spectating, making art, doing cosplay, or anything else, the Redding FGC is a place where people from all walks of life can come together to deepen their love of fighting games.

02

Raise the Level of Play in Redding

Whether you're aiming to become a pro, or you'd just like to win a little more against your friends, the Redding FGC is dedicated to providing the environment and resources necessary for everyone to improve their skills and grow as players.

03

Nurture the Talents of the Community

In the course of their work as volunteers, members of our community have built valuable skills in fields such as audio engineering, business management, public speaking, and more. As a community, we want to build an environment where people can grow professionally and open up new opportunities for themselves, even when they leave the world of fighting games.

Contact

If you would like to get involved in our community, whether that's as a visitor and participant in our events, or as a volunteer, you can connect with us through Discord and Email.
